Service policy

How Beacon Support handles your tickets: response times, business hours and who can see what.

Response times

Targets are counted in business hours from the moment a ticket is raised.

Business hours

  • Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:30 in your company's time zone (Northwind Traders: Dubai, UTC+4; Halcyon Foods: Singapore, UTC+8). Weekends don't count.
  • Public holidays don't count either. The desk observes: New Year's Day and Founders' Day.
  • Times on Beacon are shown in the requester's company time zone.

How the clocks run

  • The first-response clock stops at the first public reply from an agent. Internal notes don't count as a response.
  • The resolution clock runs until the ticket is solved. It pauses while the ticket is Waiting on customer; when you reply, the ticket goes back to Open and the clock restarts.
  • If a ticket's priority changes, the new priority's targets apply, counted from when the ticket was raised.

Raising a ticket

  • Every plan can choose Low, Normal or High. Urgent (for a service that's down) is part of the Premium plan; on the Standard plan our agents can raise a ticket to Urgent for you.
  • Subjects can be up to 120 characters; describe the problem in at least 20.
  • Attach up to 5 files of up to 10 MB each: PNG, JPG, PDF, TXT, LOG or CSV, whatever the case of the file name (screenshot.PNG is fine).
  • Copy in up to 5 colleagues. Copies can only go to addresses at your company's own email domain (for Northwind Traders, @northwind.test).

Your tickets

  • Search finds tickets by subject or by number, with or without the # (1022 or #1022).
  • "Priority" sorting lists the most urgent first: Urgent, High, Normal, Low. Lists show 5 tickets a page.
  • You can reopen a solved ticket within 7 days of it being solved. After 7 days it closes for good.
  • Closed tickets can't be replied to or reopened: raise a new ticket and mention the old number.
  • Once a ticket is solved, the person who raised it can rate it from 1 to 5 stars, once. Ratings can't be changed.
  • You can download a ticket's conversation as a text file.

Privacy

  • You see the tickets you raised. Your company's admins also see, and can reply to, every ticket their colleagues raise; only the person who raised a ticket can solve, reopen or rate it.
  • Nobody ever sees another company's tickets.
  • Agents' internal notes are never shown to customers: not on the ticket, not in emails and not in downloads.
  • "Solved this month" on the company page counts tickets solved since the 1st of this month.

Help centre

  • Search looks in article titles and article text.
  • Each person can vote once per article. The helpful score is the share of all votes that said "Yes".

For our support team

  • Queue views: Unassigned shows unsolved tickets (New, Open or Waiting) with no agent; Overdue shows unsolved tickets that have passed their first-response target without a public reply, or their resolution target.
  • Agents who are away can't be given tickets. A ticket can only be solved once the customer has had at least one public reply.
  • Escalating to Tier 2 needs a reason, adds the tier-2 tag and makes the priority at least High: Urgent tickets stay Urgent.
  • Tickets can have up to 5 tags (lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens).
  • Merging closes the duplicate and moves all of its messages into the ticket you keep. Only tickets from the same company can be merged, never into themselves or into a closed ticket.
  • Canned replies fill in {{first_name}} (the customer's first name), {{ticket_id}} (e.g. #1022) and {{agent_name}} (your first name), everywhere they appear.
  • Bulk actions only change the tickets you tick in the current view.

Reports

  • Reports cover tickets raised in the last 30 days; days run midnight to midnight Dubai time. "Tickets per day" shows the last 7 days, today included.
  • Average first response is the mean business time to the first public reply, over the tickets that have had one.
  • SLA breach rate = tickets that missed either target ÷ all tickets in the period.
  • CSAT = ratings of 4 or 5 stars ÷ all ratings.
  • The CSV download has one row per ticket and opens cleanly in a spreadsheet.
